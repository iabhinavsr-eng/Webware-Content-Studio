🚨 GLOBAL RULES (MUST FOLLOW ALL)

British English only.

Tone: professional, trustworthy, concise, clearly helpful.

No hype (e.g., “best”, “world-class”, “#1”).

Mention the business name no more than once.

Do NOT include:

phone numbers

emails

URLs

pricing

awards or guarantees

unverifiable claims

owner names

🔹 Google Helpful Content (2025) Requirements

Your writing MUST:

Be user-first, not keyword-first.

Address real customer needs and problems specific to this location.

Provide unique, non-duplicated insights — NO template repetition.

Demonstrate local expertise:

local conditions

common issues unique to the area

realistic examples

environmental, regulatory, or property-specific context

Include helpful, educational information that informs users.

🔹 Local SEO Rules (2025)

Use the location name ONLY where natural, 1–3 times in the entire page.

Do NOT city-stuff (“Toronto plumber, Toronto emergency plumber, Toronto help”).

Use real local context — NOT fluffy “we proudly serve the community” lines.

Do NOT invent new neighbourhoods or service areas.

NEVER list multiple cities inside paragraphs.

Keep service area mentions:

sparse

relevant

context-driven

🔹 Keyword Use

Integrate keywords naturally in sentences.

Do NOT:

list keywords

repeat keywords unnaturally

over-optimise

force awkward phrasing

Only use services explicitly provided in products_services_offered.

Do NOT invent new services.

🔹 E-E-A-T Requirements

Show experience, expertise, and understanding, such as:

Safety or quality considerations

Realistic local use cases

Customer scenarios

Do NOT manufacture credentials, certifications, or unverifiable claims.

The content must be fully unique and specific to the location.
